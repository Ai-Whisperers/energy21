### 1A — Corporate Structure Decision

**Two scenarios:**

#### Scenario A: All-in-One (Simple)
```
AI Whisperers (Paraguay HQ)
├── Solstein (product division)
├── Accelerators (services division)
└── Research (capabilities)
```
**Pros:** Simple, clean, one tax entity  
**Cons:** Slower scaling, less attractive to investors, ties Solstein/Services together

#### Scenario B: Multi-Entity (Recommended)
```
AI Whisperers Paraguay (HQ)
├── Solstein IP (Paraguay) [pure IP company]
└── Solstein Commercial BV (Estonia/Netherlands) [platform + sales]

+ AI Whisperers Services (EU entity)
  ├── Accelerators (services)
  └── Consulting/Research
```
**Pros:** Clean IP control, investor-friendly, tax optimized, scalable  
**Cons:** More entities, more accounting, more complexity

**→ Recommended:** Scenario B with clean separation
