## PITCH STRUCTURE (Template for any client)

### **Opening (2 min)**
*"We've analyzed your [situation]. Here's what we found: [key pain point]. Here's how we solve it: [our solution]. Expected impact: [ROI]."*

**Example for Energy21:**
"You're managing ticket triage manually (20+ hours/week), deploying code by hand (2-hour deployments), and your team is barely keeping up. We've designed three accelerators to automate this, free your team to focus on strategic work, and deliver $1.2M/year in measurable value. We'll start with a 2-week quick win (Ticket Triage), then move to deployment automation, then company-wide AI training. Total investment: $65K. Payback: ~5 months."

### **Problem Deep Dive (3 min)**
*"Tell me more about what's broken..."*
- Listen to client's specific pain
- Map their problem to one of your accelerators
- Show you understand their constraints (time, budget, team capacity)

**Example:** "So Jacqueline is spending 3 hours/day on ticket classification, which means less time on customer issues. And deployments take 2 hours because multiple people have to review and approve. Got it."

### **Solution (5 min)**
*"Here's how we fix this..."*
- Walk through the accelerator scope
- Show timeline (e.g., "2 weeks, demo in Week 1, full deployment by Week 2")
- Show deliverables (exact files, systems, training)
- Show success metrics (measurable outcomes)

**Example:** "Accelerator 1 automates ticket triage using N8N. We configure AI to read tickets, classify them, auto-fix known issues, and escalate complex ones. Jacqueline gets 20+ hours/week back. You'll see the system working in Week 1, full production by Week 2. We measure: tickets automated (target: 70%), response time (target: <5 min), Jacqueline's freed time."

### **Investment & ROI (2 min)**
*"Here's what it costs and what you get back..."*
- Show pricing (Standard/Essential/Enterprise options)
- Show ROI (savings, payback period, ongoing value)
- Show financing (upfront payment vs. installments)

**Example:** "Accelerator 1 is $20K. You'll save $52K/year in labor costs (Jacqueline's freed time). Payback in about 5 months. We offer Standard ($20K) for full scope, or Essential ($15K) if you want to minimize upfront cost."

### **Timeline (1 min)**
*"Here's when this happens..."*
- Week-by-week breakdown
- Key milestones
- Support period

**Example:** "Week 1: Assessment and initial setup. Friday: we demo the system working with your real tickets. Week 2: full production rollout. Weeks 3-8: we support you, fix issues, optimize performance. Two check-ins per week."

### **Decision (1 min)**
*"What's next?"*
- Clear next step
- Decision timeline
- Contact person

**Example:** "We can start next Monday. I'll send you a formal proposal. You have until Friday to decide. Any questions?"

## PRESENTATION TIPS

**Before you present:**
- [ ] Print the accelerator documents and bring them (clients want reference materials)
- [ ] Have pricing sheet ready (be prepared to discuss cost options)
- [ ] Know your ROI math by heart (can do it in your sleep)
- [ ] Practice the pitch aloud (60 min, no stumbles)
- [ ] Prepare 2-3 specific examples from Energy21 or other clients

**During the pitch:**
- [ ] Start with their problem, not your solution (listen first, present second)
- [ ] Use their language (if they say "deployment bottleneck", use that phrase)
- [ ] Show, don't tell (demo if possible; otherwise, detailed walkthrough)
- [ ] Emphasize outcomes, not features ("70% automated" not "N8N configuration")
- [ ] Pause for questions (don't lecture non-stop)
- [ ] Confirm next steps (don't leave ambiguous)

**After the pitch:**
- [ ] Send formal proposal (copy from template)
- [ ] Include ROI calculations (show your work)
- [ ] Include timeline (week-by-week)
- [ ] Include success metrics (what you'll measure)
- [ ] Set decision deadline (e.g., "Proposal valid through Friday")
- [ ] Follow up once (email Tuesday or Wednesday if they haven't decided)
