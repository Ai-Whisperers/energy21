Status: Draft templates for legal counsel
Audience: Legal teams, corporate counsel, notaries
Disclaimer: Templates only. Must be reviewed and finalized by licensed attorneys in each jurisdiction.

## Overview

This document contains three core agreements for the AI Whisperers three-entity structure:

1. IP Assignment Agreement (John -> Solstein OUE)
2. Exclusive License Agreement (Solstein OUE -> Solstein Commercial BV)
3. Shareholder Agreement (Michiel + Kyrian for Solstein Commercial BV)

Use placeholders where indicated and tailor to local legal requirements.

## Agreement 1: IP Assignment Agreement
### Parties
- Assignor: [FULL LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS], "Assignor"
- Assignee: Solstein OUE, [ADDRESS], "Assignee"

### Recitals
- Assignor has created or will create IP related to the Solstein platform.
- Assignee requires full ownership of IP for commercialization.

### Definitions
- "IP" includes inventions, software, source code, documentation, designs, algorithms, trademarks, patents, and trade secrets.
- "Work Product" means all deliverables created during the engagement, whether alone or jointly.

### Core Terms
1. Assignment of IP
   - Assignor assigns all right, title, and interest in IP created in scope of engagement.
2. Scope
   - Includes software, source code, documentation, designs, trademarks, patents, trade secrets.
3. Moral Rights
   - Assignor waives moral rights where allowed by law or grants broad usage rights.
4. Consideration
   - Compensation: [SALARY], [EQUITY], and/or [OTHER CONSIDERATION].
5. Warranties
   - Assignor warrants IP is original and does not infringe third-party rights.
   - Assignor warrants no open source obligations conflict with the assignment.
6. Confidentiality
   - Standard NDA obligations during and after engagement.
7. Non-Compete / Non-Solicit
   - Duration: [24-36 months], subject to local enforceability.
8. Further Assurances
   - Assignor agrees to execute additional documents to perfect the assignment.
8. Term and Termination
   - Effective date: [DATE], retroactive if required.
9. Governing Law
   - Estonia.
10. Dispute Resolution
   - Arbitration or court jurisdiction in Estonia.

- Assignee: ____________________

## Agreement 2: Exclusive License Agreement
### Parties
- Licensor: Solstein OUE, [ADDRESS], "Licensor"
- Licensee: Solstein Commercial BV, [ADDRESS], "Licensee"

### Recitals
- Licensor owns the Solstein IP.
- Licensee will commercialize the IP in client engagements.

### Definitions
- "Licensed IP" means the Solstein platform and related IP.
- "Gross Revenue" means all client revenue derived from services using the Licensed IP, net of VAT.

### Core Terms
1. Grant of License
   - Exclusive, worldwide license to use Solstein IP for commercial services.
2. Territory
   - Worldwide, with focus on EU and North America.
3. Term
   - [5 years] with auto-renewal, subject to termination clauses.
4. License Fee
   - 35 percent of gross revenue from commercial engagements.
   - Payment schedule: monthly within [X] days of month end.
   - Reporting: monthly revenue statement with supporting invoices.
5. Sublicensing
   - Allowed with prior written consent from Licensor.
6. Ownership
   - Licensor retains ownership of all IP and derivatives.
7. Quality and Brand Use
   - Licensee must maintain quality standards and follow brand usage guidelines.
7. Confidentiality
   - Mutual confidentiality obligations.
8. Audit Rights
   - Licensor may audit Licensee revenue reporting annually.
9. Termination
   - Material breach with [30-60 days] cure period.
10. Governing Law
   - Netherlands.

### Signatures
- Licensor: ____________________
- Licensee: ____________________
