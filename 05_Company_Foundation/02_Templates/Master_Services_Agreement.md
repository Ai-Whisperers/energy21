The Contractor agrees to perform software development, AI consultation, sales support, and other related services as described in separate Statements of Work (SOW) executed by both parties. Any changes to the scope defined in an SOW must be agreed upon in writing.

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in effect until terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time with thirty (30) days written notice. Payment for services rendered up to the date of termination shall be due immediately upon final invoice.
