## 2. Engine 1: Solstein (The Product)

### The Problem it Solves
Private Equity firms spend €1.5M on consultants like McKinsey to analyze markets. The consultants take 90 days and deliver a static PDF. In today's market, that information is outdated before it's printed. 

### What is Solstein?
Solstein—"The Sunstone for Capital Navigation"—is our proprietary AI intelligence platform. It analyzes dozens of companies in a specific sector simultaneously, extracting signals (tech stack, SaaS adoption, AI maturity, margins, growth) to classify them.

**The Output: The Attractiveness Board**
Every company analyzed is placed into a bucket:
- 🚀 **Rocket (≥ 7.0):** High-growth, AI-native. (Buy these).
- ⚖️ **Neutral (4.0–7.0):** Stable, needs signal interpretation.
- 🦕 **Dinosaur (≤ 4.0):** Carrying coal. (Buy cheap and let us transform them).

### The Commercial Play (How Solstein makes money)
1. **Pilot (EUR 60K):** We analyze one specific market map in 3-7 days.
2. **Subscription (EUR 75K-150K/yr):** Continuous monitoring of a vertical.
3. **Enterprise (EUR 2M-3M):** Full portfolio sweep for mega-funds.

*Crucial Detail:* With "Dinosaurs," we offer a transmutation play. We step in, deploy AI, flip the company into the future, and take an equity stake in the upside. We are not just vendors; we are alchemy partners.

We use analogies like "Wizards," "Alchemy," and "Grimoires" to build an incredible brand identity. But when it comes to the numbers, the code, and the delivery, we operate with ruthless, Wall Street-level precision.

### B. Ownership & IP
As a member of the Guild, you are building immense value.
