## Phase 3: The Alchemy Transmutations (Months 9–14)

**Goal:** Activate the "Equity for Transmutation" model with Solstein clients.

### The Transmutation Flywheel

- We approach the PE firm: *"Solstein flagged this company as a 3.4. But the underlying domain data is a goldmine. Pay us a small retainer, give us 5% upside equity, and we will deploy our consulting team to transform this company into an 8.0."*
- **Why this is genius:** The PE firm pays us to use our own software, then gives us equity when our consulting team fixes the asset. This aligns all incentives.

- Build the data moat: Solstein's algorithms must now auto-weight their formulas based on the success/failure of past PE acquisitions.
