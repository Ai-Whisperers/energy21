## The Solution

### 2-Phase Approach:

**Phase 1: AI First-Line Support**
- AI reads incoming tickets automatically
- Answers simple/FAQ questions immediately
- Attempts to fix known issues
- Only escalates complex problems to humans

**Phase 2: Smart Ticket Creation**
- For complex issues, AI creates detailed tickets with:
  - Problem description
  - How to reproduce
  - System info
  - Suggested solutions
- Routes to right human agent

*Training courses and onsite installation/setup—separate cost.*
