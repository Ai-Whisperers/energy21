# Executive Summary
## Accelerator 1: Ticket Triage / Jacqueline

**Client:** Energy21 / Eneve + W.P.G. Weiss Poll Group  
**Prepared by:** AI Whisperers

---

## What the project is

An N8N-powered automation system that handles Energy21 ticket triage, routing, and routine responses automatically. AI reads incoming tickets, classifies them, fixes what it can (or creates detailed tickets for complex issues), and routes appropriately. Jacqueline stops organizing and starts fixing. W.P.G. (Paraguay) handles Energy21 ticket triage with the system supporting their workflow.

**Deliverables:** AI classification, auto-response workflows, auto-fix for known issues, smart escalation, integration with Energy21's existing ticketing system, 60 days support, two check-ins per week with W.P.G. head during engagement.
