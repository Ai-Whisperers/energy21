## Executive Summary

AI Whisperers uses a three-entity structure to optimize operational efficiency and tax outcomes:

- AI Whisperers Holding (Paraguay) - operational HQ and cost center
- Solstein OUE (Estonia) - IP ownership and licensing entity
- Solstein Commercial BV (Netherlands) - client-facing commercial operations

The core mechanism is an arm's length licensing arrangement where the Commercial BV pays a 35 percent gross revenue license fee to Solstein OUE. This aligns the IP value with revenue generation while keeping operational activities in the Netherlands and cost-efficient back office operations in Paraguay.

## Structure and Revenue Flow

1. Client contracts signed by Solstein Commercial BV (Netherlands).
2. Client invoices issued by Commercial BV with VAT treatment per jurisdiction.
3. Commercial BV pays a monthly IP license fee to Solstein OUE (Estonia) equal to 35 percent of gross revenue.
4. Solstein OUE retains profits for reinvestment, distributing dividends only when needed.
5. AI Whisperers Holding (Paraguay) operates as a cost center, with intercompany service charges as appropriate.

## Estonia Tax Strategy (Solstein OUE)

### Corporate Tax Overview
- Estonia taxes distributed profits, not retained earnings.
- Reinvested earnings are taxed at 0 percent until distributed.
- Distributed profits are taxed at the applicable rate in effect at the time of distribution.
- Use Estonian structure to retain IP profits for reinvestment and product development.

### IP Ownership and Licensing
- Register Solstein OUE as the IP owner (software, trademarks, branding).
- License IP to the Netherlands Commercial BV under an arm's length agreement.
- Maintain robust IP documentation and proof of ownership.

### Profit Distribution
- Distributions are taxed when dividends are paid. Plan dividend timing to optimize cash flow.

### Filing Requirements
- Annual financial statements and corporate filings through the Estonia e-residency system.
- Maintain transfer pricing documentation for cross-border licensing.

### Substance and Governance
- Maintain board minutes and decision records in Estonia.
- Ensure core IP management activities are evidenced in Estonia (strategy, oversight, licensing approvals).

### Action Items
- Confirm e-residency compliance and local accountant support.
- Maintain clear evidence of IP development and ownership transfers.
