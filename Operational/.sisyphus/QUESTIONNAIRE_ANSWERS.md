### Q9: How should Solstein be licensed to Commercial BV?
**Answer: Exclusive License Agreement (30-40% Revenue Share)**

**Terms:**
- **Licensor:** Solstein OÜ (Estonia)
- **Licensee:** Solstein Commercial BV (Netherlands)
- **Territory:** EU + North America (exclusive)
- **Revenue Share:** 35% of gross revenue (middle of 30-40% range)
- **Duration:** Perpetual, terminable with 60 days notice
- **Sublicensing:** Allowed with approval

**Example:**
```
Commercial BV earns €100K from clients
↓
Pays €35K to Solstein OÜ (35% licensing fee)
↓
Retains €65K for operations (salaries, marketing, Michiel's share, taxes)
↓
Solstein OÜ earns €35K, reinvests at 0% tax (Estonia advantage)
```

### Q10: Can Solstein be licensed to other entities?

- Solstein OÜ retains right to license to other entities in different territories

- Maximizes IP value without cannibalizing existing license
