## Proposal 1: Accelerator 1 - Ticket Triage Automation

### Executive Summary
This proposal outlines a two-week implementation to automate support ticket routing and resolution for [CLIENT_NAME].

### Problem Statement
- Manual ticket triage creates slow response times.
- High-value issues are delayed due to misrouting.

### Proposed Solution
- N8N-based automation with AI classification.
- Skill-based routing and auto-resolution flows.

### Scope of Work
- Discovery and workflow mapping
- Classification rules and automation
- Integration with ticketing system
- Testing and go-live support

### Timeline and Milestones
- Week 1: Architecture, integrations, initial automation
- Week 2: Testing, refinement, launch

### Investment and ROI
- Investment: $20K (range $15K to $25K)
- ROI: $52K per year (2.6x Year 1)

### Success Metrics
- 80 percent automation rate
- 5 to 10x faster response time
- 95 percent classification accuracy

### Terms and Conditions
- Payment: 50 percent upfront, 50 percent on completion
- Change requests handled via written scope change
### Assumptions and Dependencies
- Client provides access to ticketing system and current workflows.
- Stakeholders are available for weekly review sessions.
### Out of Scope
- Custom feature development beyond agreed scope.
- Long-term support beyond the initial launch window.
