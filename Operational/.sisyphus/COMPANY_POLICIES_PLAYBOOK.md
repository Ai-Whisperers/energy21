# COMPANY POLICIES PLAYBOOK

Status: Draft
Audience: Internal team

## Decision-Making Framework

### Unanimous Decisions
- Equity changes
- Major financing or debt
- Entity restructuring

### Kyrian Domain (Commercial)
- Client relationships
- Pricing and commercial terms
- Sales pipeline priorities

### Ivan Domain (Strategic)
- Company strategy
- Partnerships and investor relations
- Long-term roadmap

### John Domain (Technical)
- Architecture decisions
- Technical standards
- Delivery quality

---

## Communication Protocols

- Weekly team sync: 30 minutes
- Client check-ins: weekly or bi-weekly
- Escalation: notify Ivan for strategic risks or contract issues

---

## Financial Policies

- Expense approval limits to be defined per entity
- Invoicing: Commercial BV issues client invoices
- Payment terms: standard 50/50 unless otherwise agreed
- Procurement: require written approval for commitments above [AMOUNT]
- Banking: dual approval for transfers above [AMOUNT]

---

## HR Policies

- Remote-first with clear availability windows
- Time off and holidays aligned with local jurisdiction
- Confidentiality and IP obligations apply to all team members
- Contractors must sign IP assignment and NDA before starting work

---

## Client Policies

- SLAs and support windows defined in contracts
- Change requests require written approval
- Data access limited to necessary systems

## Security and Data Handling

- Use least-privilege access for client systems
- Store client data only in approved tools
- Report incidents within 24 hours to Ivan

## Conflict of Interest

- Disclose potential conflicts before engaging new clients
- Avoid working with direct competitors without mutual approval
