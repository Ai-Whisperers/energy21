# 🗺️ The Captain's Dashboard: Master Setup Checklist
*A definitive validation checklist for the person in charge to ensure the company is legally, financially, and operationally bulletproof.*

**Instructions:** Do not proceed to €1M+ scaling phases or pitch PE firms for massive cross-company deals until every box below is unequivocally checked.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Corporate & Legal Infrastructure
- [ ] **Jurisdictional Clarity:** Are the 3 entities (Paraguay W.P.G., EU Interface, Solstein Holding) legally registered and active?
- [ ] **IP Assignment (CIIAA):** Has every founder, contractor, or employee signed an agreement transferring their code/algorithms to the company? *(If no, the company is un-fundable and cannot be acquired)*
- [ ] **Vesting Schedules:** Are all founders and key equity holders on a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff? 
- [ ] **Master Services Agreements (MSAs):** Do we have signed MSAs governing the relationship and cash flow between our own entities (e.g., Estonia paying Paraguay for services)?
- [ ] **GDPR / Data Privacy:** Is there a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) in place for European clients and is client data stored on EU servers?

## 💰 2. Financial Stack & Banking
- [ ] **Banking Segregation:** Are business expenses 100% segregated from personal expenses? (No piercing the corporate veil).
- [ ] **Optimal Banking Setup:** Are we using borderless financial tools capable of receiving EU PE funds smoothly? (e.g., Wise Business, Mercury, Brex, or LHV for the Estonian OÜ).
- [ ] **Clean Cap Table:** Is our Capitalization Table (who owns what percentage of the company) managed on software like Carta or Pulley, and entirely off Excel?
- [ ] **83(b) Elections (If US/Delaware C-Corp):** Did founders file their 83(b) elections within 30 days of receiving their restricted stock to avoid crushing tax liabilities later? 

## 🚀 3. Go-To-Market & Solstein Validation
- [ ] **Anchor Case Study Secured:** Have we successfully completed the Solstein pilot, delivered the 29-company "Attractiveness Board," and validated our scoring against the PE firm’s actual deal outcomes?
- [ ] **Category Design Pitch Ready:** Is our sales pitch focused on *Intelligence-as-a-Service (IaaS) / Algorithmic Deal Sourcing* rather than competing on features with basic data scraping tools?
- [ ] **"The Index" Prepared:** Do we have our targeted piece of free intelligence (The Index) ready to send to specific PE partners as a Trojan Horse?
- [ ] **Pricing Discipline:** Are we resolutely maintaining our €150K+/yr enterprise pricing to signal premium capital infrastructure, refusing to discount into the "cheap SaaS" tier?

## 📂 4. Due Diligence (The Data Room)
*When a PE firm or acquirer says "Send us the Data Room," you must have it ready in 1 hour.*
- [ ] **Data Room Fully Updated:** Are all documents in the `Data_Room_Index.md` template uploaded to a secure, organized DocSend or Google Workspace Drive?
- [ ] **Corporate Documents:** Articles, Bylaws, Good Standing Certificate, Cap Table.
- [ ] **Financials:** P&L, Balance Sheet, Projections.
- [ ] **Legal/IP:** Executed CIIAAs, trademark filings, OS license audits.
- [ ] **Material Contracts:** Signed pilot agreements, MSA with Energy21.

## ⚙️ 5. Operational Readiness (The Consulting Engine)
- [ ] **Accelerator 1 ROI Proven:** Has the Energy21 Ticket Triage system measurably reduced resolution times by 50%+?
- [ ] **Land and Expand Executed:** Have we leveraged the success of Accelerator 1 to close Accelerator 2 (Build Pipeline) and the long-term Retainer (Accelerator 3)?
- [ ] **Transmutation Pipeline:** Have we identified the first "Dinosaur" company from Solstein analysis where we can pitch a consulting transformation for 5-10% equity?

---

*If all boxes are checked, you are operating at the level of YCombinator's top 1%. Deploy the spells.*
