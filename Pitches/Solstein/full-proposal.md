### The Five Core Capabilities

**I. Market Topography (Vector Mapping)**
Solstein identifies every meaningful player in a target market. By using semantic vector mapping, it finds competitors that traditional keyword searches miss. It links companies based on underlying technological similarities, not just their marketing copy.

**II. Dimensional Vector Profiling**
Every company is profiled across eight structural dimensions: Growth Trajectory, Liquidity Cushion, Margin Physics, AI Maturity, Tech-Stack Integrity, and Organizational Velocity. 
*The Depth:* We don't read press releases. The AI analyzes structural signals—repository commit frequency, protocol certifications, engineering-to-sales hiring ratios, and historical funding debt. We look at the architectural bones of the asset.

**III. Scoring Methodology**
Three scores are calculated for each company:

| Score | What It Measures |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Growth Score (0–10)** | Revenue momentum, trajectory, margin sustainability |
| **Financial Health Score (0–10)** | Scale, funding cushion, operational efficiency |
| **Competitive Position Score (0–10)** | AI readiness, SaaS maturity, tech depth, market presence |

**IV. The Verdict (Algorithmic Classification)**
Every company receives a mathematically defensible classification:

| Classification | Score | Algorithmic Definition |
|---------------|-------|------------------------|
| **Rocket** | ≥ 7.0 | High-velocity compounding. Asymmetric upside. AI-native architecture. Will command acquisition premiums. |
| **Neutral** | 4.0–7.0 | Stagnant physics. The intelligence here isolates whether momentum is building or decaying. |
| **Dinosaur** | ≤ 4.0 | Carrying massive technical debt. Archaic infrastructure. *Highly lucrative targets for distressed buyout and automated restructuring.* |
