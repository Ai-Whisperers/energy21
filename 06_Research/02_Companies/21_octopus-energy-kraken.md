## Kraken Platform

### What is Kraken?
Kraken is an AI-powered end-to-end operating system for energy utilities.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Customer Service:** 40% lower cost to serve
- **AI/ML:** Advanced data and machine learning
- **Billing:** Automated billing and payments
- **Trading:** Energy trading integration
- **Flexibility:** Virtual power plant, grid management
- **Customer Self-Service:** High automation

### Platform Metrics:
- **Accounts Powered:** 50M+ worldwide (as of 2025)
- **Target:** 100M accounts by 2027
- **Licensees:** EDF, E.ON, Origin Energy, Good Energy, Tokyo Gas, and many more

### Recent News (Dec 2025):
- Kraken spinoff at $8.65B valuation
- $1B stake sold to D1 Capital Partners
- Plans for potential IPO
